About

Welcome to my website. I'm Elija Perrier. I'm an interdisciplinary mathematical physicist and researcher specialising in quantum–classical artificial general intelligence and superintelligent systems integration, multi‑agent modelling and AI alignment using optimal control theory. A bit more about me:

  • I publish research on quantum machine learning, AGI and superintelligence, AI risk measurement and governance, and multi‑agent systems (30+ publications, 400+ citations).
  • I completed my PhD in quantum machine learning at the University of Technology, Sydney and my other Bachelor degrees at the University of Sydney, NSW, and Murdoch University in Perth, Western Australia.
  • I have a multi-disciplinary background spanning physics, computer science, mathematics, economics, law and philosophy.
  • I've been a lawyer for over 20 years as a practitioner and legal academic across Australia, Europe and the UK, and have worked as a senior manager in investment banking.
  • I'm also a software engineer proficient in a range of frontend and backend languages (Python mostly), stack developer (AWS) and HPC/Singularity.

Current activities

I currently consult as an AI Researcher & via my company Measure Theory Analytics, providing developer, engineering and transactional expertise for clients. I am a research affiliate at Cambridge Law School, University of Cambridge, researching the governance and legal implications of quantum and AI technologies and a fellow at the Centre for Quantum Software the Centre for Quantum Software and Information at UTS, Sydney (where I completed my PhD), where I research quantum computing, AI alignment and autonomous agents (including quantum agents). I am also a research fellow with the Gradient Institute working on AI alignment and control/measurement.

Key Projects

  • Foundations of Artificial General Intelligence: Research on foundational aspects of AGI, including physical theories of general intelligence, algorithmic thermodynamics of intelligent systems and recursive self-modification of AGI.
  • AI Agent Infrastructure & Alignment: Developing foundational frameworks for deploying AI agents at scale, including evaluation methods and control‑theory‑based models for AI alignment.
  • Measurement Science for AI Risk / Mechanistic Interpretability / Economics of AGI: Applying statistical risk analysis and measurement theory to quantify AI‑specific risk (collaborating with SaferAI and Gradient Institute), plus published work on the economic impacts of AGI on fundamental welfare theorems.
  • Quantum Agents Framework: Comprehensive framework combining quantum information theory with reinforcement learning for universal intelligence, demonstrating theoretical differences between quantum agents and classical counterparts.
  • Multi‑Agent Political Simulation Platform: Designed a production‑scale LLM‑powered microsimulation system modelling national population dynamics and real‑time electoral prediction.
  • Quantum Geometric Machine Learning: Developed new techniques combining differential geometry with quantum computing, including novel quantum neural network architectures and large‑scale quantum datasets.
